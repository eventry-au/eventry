Our story

Built by athletes,
for athletes

Eventry started with a simple frustration — finding your next sporting event shouldn't be hard. So we built the place we always wished existed.

Where it started

The problem with finding events

You cross a finish line. You're exhausted, exhilarated, already wondering — what's next? You pull out your phone and start searching. You visit five different websites, scroll through Facebook groups, check a few club newsletters. Half the events are out of date. Some links are broken. Half the ones you find have already closed registrations.

Sound familiar? We thought so. Australia has thousands of incredible sporting events every year — trail runs, triathlons, ocean swims, obstacle races, cycling tours, paddling classics. But finding them, comparing them, and actually entering them has always been more work than it should be.

"Every race, ride, paddle and plunge — all in one place. That's what we set out to build."

The name

Event + Entry = Eventry

The name says exactly what we do. Finding sporting events and entering them. Two words, one mission, one name.

Event + Entry = Eventry
Find it. Enter it. Do it.

We wanted a name that was simple, memorable, and meant something. Eventry isn't just a word we made up — it's a description of what happens here. You find an event. You enter it. That's Eventry.

What we believe

Sport is for everyone

We believe every Australian — whether they're chasing a podium or just want to finish — deserves easy access to the events that challenge and inspire them. From kids' fun runs to 100-mile ultras. From local paddle races to multi-day mountain bike epics.

🏁
