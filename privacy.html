Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Eventry ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you use eventry.au. We are committed to handling your information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

By using this site you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Who we are

Eventry is an Australian sports events directory operated by Adriaan Moore, based in New South Wales, Australia.

Contact: eventry.au@gmail.com

What information we collect

Information you give us directly:

  • Email address — when you subscribe to our newsletter
  • Event details, contact name, phone number and email address — when you submit an event listing via our submission form

Information collected automatically:

When you visit eventry.au we automatically collect certain technical information through Google Analytics, including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Your approximate location (country and city level, derived from your IP address)
  • Device type, browser and operating system
  • How you found the site (search engine, direct link, referral)

Your IP address is processed by Google but is not stored by us. We do not collect precise location data.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send you our newsletter, if you have subscribed (email address only)
  • Review and publish event listings you submit
  • Contact event organisers about their listings
  • Understand how the site is used so we can improve it
  • Comply with legal obligations

We will never use your information for purposes other than those described above, and we will never sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies and analytics

Eventry uses Google Analytics 4 to measure site traffic and understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to collect this information.

What this means for you:

  • Google Analytics sets cookies when you visit the site, subject to your consent via our cookie banner
  • Anonymised usage data is sent to Google's servers and may be stored outside Australia
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking at any time by clicking "Decline" on our cookie banner, or by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on

We do not use cookies for advertising, retargeting or any purpose other than analytics.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Google — for analytics purposes, as described above. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy
  • Law enforcement or regulators — if required by Australian law

Event listing information submitted through our form (event name, organiser name, location, dates) is published publicly on the site. Contact details (email, phone) submitted with a listing are stored by us and used only for administrative purposes — they are not displayed publicly.

How we store and protect your information

Your information is stored securely in Google's infrastructure (Google Sheets and Google Workspace), which is subject to Google's security standards. We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access.

Newsletter email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe. Organiser contact details are retained while their listings remain active on the site. You may request deletion at any time.

Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal and operational requirements)
  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time by emailing us or using the unsubscribe link in any newsletter
  • Opt out of analytics by declining cookies via our cookie banner

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at eventry.au@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle a privacy concern, you can lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Contact us

For any privacy-related questions or requests:

Email: eventry.au@gmail.com
Website: eventry.au

This policy may be updated from time to time. If we make significant changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
